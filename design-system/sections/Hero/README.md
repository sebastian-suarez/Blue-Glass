# Hero

The opening section of the site: a display headline and lede beside a diagram on glass, one primary action and one glass action.

**Composed from:** the `text-display` and `text-lede` styles, `Button` (`primary`, `glass`, `text` with `arrow-ne`), and `Paper` holding a `Diagram` of the site's own deploy flow. Two columns from 900px and one below, as in `global.css`.

## How to reuse it

- One `primary` action ("Email me"), one `glass` action ("See the work").
- Headline: a claim in two short sentences, at most 11ch per line. Lede: one or two sentences in `color-muted`, at most 42ch.
- The figure always carries a caption that says what the diagram shows, plus a "go" link.
